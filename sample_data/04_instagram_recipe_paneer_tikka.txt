Title: Restaurant-Style Smoky Paneer Tikka in an Oven / Air Fryer 🧀🔥

Ingredients:
- 300g fresh Malai Paneer (cubed large)
- 1 cup hung curd / Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1 tsp roasted kasuri methi (crushed)
- 1/2 tsp chaat masala
- 1 tbsp roasted besan (gram flour) for binding
- 1 tbsp mustard oil (heated until smoking)
- Diced capsicum and red onions

Instructions:
1. Whisk hung curd with spices, roasted besan, and hot mustard oil to create the marinade.
2. Gently fold in the paneer cubes, capsicum, and onions. Rest in fridge for 45 minutes.
3. Skewer and bake at 200°C for 12-14 minutes in oven or 10 mins in air fryer until edges char slightly.
4. Baste with melted butter, sprinkle lemon juice and chaat masala. Serve hot with mint chutney!
